Basic Salsa with Any Kind of Dry Chiles

Recipe By : Patricia Wriedt - Mexico City

6 large Chiles dry *
1/2 medium Onion
1/4 cup Vinegar
1 clove garlic
Salt
Vegetable oil

* Morita, mulato, guajillo or any kind.

Wash the chiles in water and discard the seeds and threads of chiles.
