**Delicious Homemade Pizza**

**Ingredients:**

- 2 cups flour
- 1 tsp active dry yeast

- 3/4 cup warm water
- 2 tbsp olive oil

- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 cup mozzarella cheese, shredded

**Directions:**

1. Combine flour, yeast, and salt in a bowl.

2. Add warm water and olive oil, stirring until a dough forms.
3. Knead for 5 minutes and let rise for 1 hour.

4. Top with cheese and bake at 450F for 15 minutes.
