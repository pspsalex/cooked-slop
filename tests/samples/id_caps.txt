 100001 -- SAMPLE ID CAPS RECIPE

 --CRUST:--

1 1/4 c. graham cracker crumbs
1/2 c. diet margarine

--FILLING:--

1 envelope unflavored gelatin
1 packet low-calorie whipped topping
   mix
1 1/2 c. skim milk

 Combine crumbs with diet margarine. Press into pie pan and bake for 10 minutes. Prepare topping mix according to package directions. Spoon over crust and chill until set.

------------------------

 100002 -- SIMPLE SECOND RECIPE

 2 eggs, beaten
1/2 c. sugar
1 c. flour

 Mix all ingredients together in a large bowl. Pour into greased pan and bake at 350 degrees for 25 minutes.
