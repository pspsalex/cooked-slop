QTitle: Rice Pilaf Yield: 2 servings

1 cup long-grain rice                  2 cups chicken broth
1 tbsp butter                          1/2 tsp salt
1 pinch black pepper \- ground

---- RECIPE ----

In a saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat.

--------

Add the rice and cook until lightly toasted, about 3 minutes.
Pour in the chicken broth, salt, and black pepper, bring to a boil.
Cover, reduce heat to low, and simmer for 18 minutes.
