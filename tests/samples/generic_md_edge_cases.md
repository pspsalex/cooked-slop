**1-2-3 Peanut Butter Cookies**

1\. cup peanut butter

2\. 1/2 cup sugar

3\. 1 large egg

Pre-heat oven to 325 degrees. Mix egg and sugar until creamy. Add peanut butter and mix well. Bake for 12 minutes.

**Beef Stroganoff**

**1 lb. ground beef**

**1 onion (size does not matter but not too small)**

**1 cup sliced mushrooms**

**1 can Campbells Cream of Chicken Soup**

**1/2 cup sour cream**

**Egg Noodles**

**Cook ground beef and onion. Add soup and mushrooms.**

**Heat through. Add sour cream. Salt and pepper to taste.**

**Serve over cooked egg noodles.**

1\. ALDILLA

1 large onion \-- chopped

1 1/2 pound flank steak

Score steak and rub with chili powder; coat with flour. Cook on low for 8 hours.
