**Chocolate Fudge Brownies**

Delish chocolate brownies!

**Ingredients:**

- 1 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup butter
- 2 eggs
- 1/3 cup cocoa powder

**Directions:**

1. Melt butter and mix with sugar.
2. Stir in eggs and cocoa powder.

3. Bake at 350F for 20 minutes.

**Simple Garlic Bread**

Great side for pasta!

**Ingredients:**

- 1 loaf French bread
- 4 tbsp butter
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

**Directions:**

1. Slice bread in half.
2. Spread garlic butter over bread.

3. Broil for 5 minutes until golden.
