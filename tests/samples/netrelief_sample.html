Artichoke Dip Appetizer

I downloaded this recipe off the internet years ago.

Garry

Recipe By : jbilos@labs-n.bbn.com (John Bilos)
Serving Size : 10

8 ounces cream cheese
12 ounces mozzarella cheese -- shredded
1 cup mayonnaise
1 cup grated parmesan cheese
1 onion -- finely chopped
2 cloves garlic -- finely chopped
2 small jars marinated artichoke hearts -- DRAIN WELL
2 bags pita bread

Tear artichokes apart with your fingers.

Combine all other ingredients and mash.

Bake in an uncovered casserole dish for approx 30 minutes @ 350 or until bubbly.
